What the percentages mean

These are Steam global unlock rates — the share of everyone who owns the game that has the achievement. They are a difficulty proxy, not a guide: First Blood sits at 96.6% because it unlocks for killing your first customer, which nearly everyone does by accident.

The three hidden achievements — Grave Decision (26.0%), True Ending (13.4%) and Empty Home (7.0%) — do not show their requirements in-game, and we have not verified them. We list what the rates imply on the endings page rather than publishing a guess as fact.
